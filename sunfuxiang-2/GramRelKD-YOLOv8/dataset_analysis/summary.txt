G. Most likely data-side causes of moisture's poor detection (ranked by the strength of evidence above):
   1) SEVERE class imbalance (strongest): only 1009 moisture instances vs 13722 crack / 2078 spalling (13.6x imbalance, see A). Far fewer positive examples -> weakest supervision; this alone plausibly accounts for much of the gap.

   5) Border location (negligible): see C -- moisture touches the border only marginally more often; not a meaningful differentiator.

NOTE: these are data-statistics observations, not model-error analysis. They identify the most plausible contributors; confirming each requires per-image inspection (montages) and/or model confusion-matrix analysis.
