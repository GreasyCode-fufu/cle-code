## Grading recipe identical to R14

Reuse immutable frozen_feature_grader.py and fixed public R9 DINO CLS+patch-mean768 features (columns512:1280 of2386x1280). No neural grader update; public pretraining overlap remains unaudited. For each outer job grade only its train/fit patients: original inner and outer excluded from every head fit, transformation and grade assignment. Inside fit, SGKF5 xreplicates42/43/44, random_state=outer_seed*100000+outer_fold*100+replicate, each fit image receives3 held-patient predictions.15 units/225 convex LogisticRegression fits; these are not225 neural trainings.

Head standardization mean/std ddof0/floor1e-6 only from head-fit patients. Multinomial C1/l1_ratio0/lbfgs/intercept/tol1e-6/maxiter2000, no class weights, no hyperparameter or probability calibration search. Convergence warning or gradient residual>5e-5 stops for explicit recorded repair. Save head parameters, transformations,fit/held indices and SHAs; exact finite saved heads reused on restart.

Use original core.grade unchanged: three predictions' correctness fraction and mean probability of EXISTING label; A both>=.95,B both>=.85,E either<.30,D either<.60,otherwiseC. No grades outside outer fit. It is a fixed consistency proxy, not human quality truth, exact old CNN grading equivalence, or proof of mislabels. The fixed source features are generated by an encoder with no CLE supervised update.
